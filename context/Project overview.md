# AutoCare – Project Overview

## 1. Executive Summary
**AutoCare** is a centralized, role-based Vehicle Service Management System engineered to modernize vehicle workshop operations. It bridges communication gaps among vehicle owners, service technicians, and garage management, transforming manual automotive operations into a synchronized, transparent, and digital workflow.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Traditional automotive service centers and workshops frequently rely on manual, paper-driven workflows, which lead to significant operational bottlenecks:

- **Manual Record-Keeping & Loss of Data:** Physical job cards, handwritten service histories, and paper receipts are prone to misplacement, damage, and human error.
- **Lack of Transparency for Vehicle Owners:** Customers have no visibility into the real-time stage of repair or maintenance without repeatedly calling service advisors.
- **Inefficient Job Tracking & Allocation:** Garage managers struggle to monitor bay availability, technician workloads, and parts consumption in real-time.
- **Billing & Invoice Discrepancies:** Manual compilation of labor charges and spare parts often results in inaccurate invoices and customer disputes.
- **Poor Customer Retention:** Absence of automated reminders for routine maintenance intervals (e.g., oil changes, seasonal checkups) leads to missed business opportunities.

---

## 3. Proposed Solution
AutoCare delivers a responsive web platform that digitizes the entire automotive servicing lifecycle. The platform implements secure, role-based access control with distinct portals for **Customers**, **Staff (Service Technicians / Advisors)**, and **Administrators**.

### 3.1 Role-Based Functional Specifications

#### 🚗 Customer Portal
- **Account Management:** User registration, profile updates, and secure authentication.
- **Vehicle Profiles:** Register multiple vehicles with details (Make, Model, Year, VIN/Chassis Number, License Plate).
- **Service Booking:** Schedule service appointments with preferred dates, time slots, and selected service packages.
- **Real-Time Tracking:** Live service status monitoring (e.g., *Booked*, *Under Inspection*, *In Progress*, *Quality Check*, *Ready for Pickup*).
- **Service History & Invoices:** Digital repository of past service visits, itemized repair breakdown, and downloadable invoices.
- **Feedback & Ratings:** Provide ratings and reviews on completed jobs.

#### 🔧 Staff / Technician Portal
- **Task Assignment Board:** View allocated service jobs with vehicle specifications and customer-reported issues.
- **Status Updates:** Transition job states through standardized workflow stages.
- **Service Logging:** Log parts replaced, consumable usage, hours worked, and technician inspection notes.
- **Job Completion:** Mark repairs complete and hand off to quality check / billing.

#### 📊 Administrator Portal
- **Executive Dashboard:** Overview of daily appointments, active repairs, revenue metrics, and shop capacity.
- **User & Staff Management:** Manage customer accounts and staff credentials, roles, and shift assignments.
- **Service Catalog Management:** Configure services, standard pricing, labor rates, and package bundles.
- **Appointment Scheduling & Job Allocation:** Approve or reschedule booking requests and assign jobs to available technicians.
- **Billing & Invoicing:** Generate comprehensive, itemized invoices factoring in parts, labor, and taxes.
- **Reports & Analytics:** Analytical reports on revenue, frequent service types, technician performance, and turnaround times.

---

## 4. Expected Benefits & Outcomes
- **Operational Efficiency:** Minimizes paperwork and reduces turnaround times through digitized workflows.
- **Customer Satisfaction:** Builds trust through end-to-end transparency and accessible digital service history.
- **Data Integrity & Security:** Centralized database storage eliminates data duplication and prevents record loss.
- **Scalability:** Modular architecture allows future enhancements such as payment gateway integrations, SMS notifications, and predictive maintenance alerts.
